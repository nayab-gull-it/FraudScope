Detection engine

What FraudScope actually checks for

Digit pattern

Benford's Law deviation

Naturally occurring numbers follow a predictable first-digit distribution. Fabricated figures rarely do.

Outliers

Statistical anomalies

Z-score and IQR analysis surface transactions that sit far outside the normal range for that account.

Repetition

Duplicate & near-duplicate detection

Catches the same transaction entered twice, or entered again with a slightly altered amount.

Rounding

Round-number bias

Real transactions are messy. A cluster of suspiciously round figures is a known fabrication signal.

Structuring

Threshold-skirting

Flags transactions clustered just under an approval limit — a classic sign of deliberate evasion.

Timing

Time-based anomalies

Unusual activity outside business hours or in weekend bursts gets surfaced automatically.

Privacy by design

Your ledger never has to leave the room.

Every detection method runs locally against your file. If you turn on AI-written summaries, only anonymized, aggregated statistics are sent out — never raw rows, names, or account numbers. Prefer nothing to leave at all? Flip on Offline Mode and skip AI summaries entirely.
